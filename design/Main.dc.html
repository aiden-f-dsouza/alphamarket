Three steps. Nothing to trust.

Full method on the methodology page
01Connect

Paste a read-only Kalshi API key. We check its scope before we store it: a key that can place orders is rejected on the spot. The private key is encrypted at rest and erased the moment you disconnect.

02Reconcile

Every fill and settlement is pulled straight from the venue into an append-only ledger. Each night we replay it and compare the computed cash against your actual Kalshi balance. If they diverge, the page says so.

03Publish

Your public page shows only what we computed: equity curve, drawdown, monthly returns, risk score, behavioural flags. The one thing you write, your thesis, sits in a box labelled unverified.

What a page proves, and what it doesn't.

A verified page is a narrow claim, made precisely. We'd rather you know the edges than believe the whole thing.

Verified
Every trade on the page happened on Kalshi, in this account.
Returns are net of venue fees, to the sub-cent.
The equity curve matched the venue balance last night.
The connected key cannot place, cancel or fund anything.
Not verified
That the trader will keep winning.
Anything the trader typed, including the thesis.
Activity older than the venue's fill history allows.
Positions held on any other venue.
