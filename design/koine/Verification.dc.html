Verification

How a Koine record is computed.

This page is the contract behind the word verified. Every formula here has a unit test with hand-computed expected values, and the same code produces every number on every strategy page.

What a read-only key can do

A creator connects a venue API key scoped to read. Before Koine stores it, the key is tested against the venue: if it can place, cancel or fund anything, it is refused. The key is encrypted with a server-side vault key, never logged, never returned by any page or API, and erased the moment the creator disconnects.

The append-only ledger

Fills and settlements are pulled from the venue into a ledger that only accepts new rows. Nothing is ever updated or deleted; a correction is a new row. Every monetary value is stored as integer micro-dollars, because venues report sub-cent fees and rounding them would corrupt the curve that reconciliation has to match.

fill: venue_fill_id, market, side, count, price, fee, filled_at settlement: venue_settlement_id, market, revenue, fee, settled_at idempotency: unique venue ids. Re-importing is a no-op.

Nightly reconciliation

Each night Koine replays the ledger from the balance recorded at connect time and compares the result with the balance the venue reports. A match is recorded on the page. A divergence quarantines the page until it is explained and matches again; it is never quietly absorbed.

Returns and the equity curve

Equity on a day is cash plus open positions at cost, net of every venue fee. Returns are time-weighted, so a creator's own deposits and withdrawals do not show up as performance.

equity_t = cash_t + Σ open_positions_at_cost_t r_t = equity_t / (equity_(t-1) + net_flow_t) − 1 TWR = Π (1 + r_t) − 1

Drawdown

Drawdown is measured from the running peak of the equity curve at daily UTC close. The worst drawdown, its dates and the days to recover are shown on every page at the same visual weight as the return.

dd_t = equity_t / max(equity_0..t) − 1 worst = min(dd_t) recovery = days from trough to new peak

Sharpe, Sortino and the risk score

Sharpe and Sortino are computed on daily returns and annualised. Both are shown with the length of the record, because a ratio built on a few weeks of data says more about luck than skill. The risk score is a 1 to 10 rank of realised behaviour only: volatility, worst drawdown, position concentration and the size of the largest single loss. It never uses anything the creator wrote.

sharpe = mean(r_t) / stdev(r_t) × √365 sortino = mean(r_t) / stdev(r_t where r_t < 0) × √365 risk = rank over volatility, worst dd, concentration, largest loss → 1..10

Behavioural flags

Three patterns are checked nightly and shown on the page when found: martingale sizing (stakes that grow after losses), sudden stake spikes relative to the trailing average, and concentration of the book in a single market series. A flag does not remove a strategy; it is printed beside the return so a reader sees it before the number.

Incubation and the Koine score

A new strategy is listed as incubating for its first 90 verified days and cannot score above 5.0, however good the return. The Koine score that ranks the marketplace weights Sharpe and Sortino most, then the share of profitable months, then worst drawdown and length of record. It is designed so that a longer, steadier record outranks a shorter, hotter one.

score = 10 × w_1·f(sharpe, sortino) + w_2·profitable_months + w_3·g(worst_dd) + w_4·h(days) incubating (days < 90): score = min(score, 5.0)

What we cannot verify

Anything the creator wrote. Positions on venues that are not connected. Activity older than the venue's history returns. Whether the record will continue. A verified page is a narrow claim about what happened in one account; treat it that way.
