Before you tail: five questions
Is the verified window longer than the strategy's worst drawdown took to recover?
Would you hold through the worst drawdown shown, at your allocation?
Is the return concentrated in one month or one market series?
How much of the estimated capacity is already taken?
Does the creator's description promise anything the numbers do not show?
Every answer is on the strategy page. If one is not, that is the answer.
Glossary
Worst drawdownLargest fall from a peak in the equity curve, at daily close.
SharpeReturn per unit of volatility, annualised. Meaningless under ~90 days.
High-water markThe highest value your allocation has reached. Fees apply only above it.
CapacityEstimated capital a strategy can absorb before its own trades move the price.
IncubationThe first 90 verified days. Listed, but cannot rank above 5.0.
