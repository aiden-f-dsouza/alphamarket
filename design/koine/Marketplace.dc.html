How the score works

Sharpe and Sortino carry most of the weight. Consistency (share of profitable months), worst drawdown and verified days adjust it. A strategy under 90 verified days is incubating and cannot rank above 5.0 regardless of return. Full formula on the Verification page.

Why losers are listed

Strategies that are losing money stay on the marketplace with their record intact. Hiding them would make the winners look like the whole population. See platform-wide outcomes on the Transparency page.
