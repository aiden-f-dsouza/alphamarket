- Thoughts on the website so far: I would like slightly lighter color. Also add a page or smth where people can send invite people to the website → call out creators/ known people who invest 
- Homepage: Lead with “Invest in traders who can prove it.” Immediately communicate the four core points: track records are verified from venue data, investor money stays in the investor’s own account, strategies remain private, and creators are paid only when investors profit. The main visual should be a verified strategy card showing return, worst drawdown, Sharpe ratio, verified history, capacity, and a performance chart. Verification should be more prominent than returns.
- Marketplace: Let users discover strategies across stocks, crypto, prediction markets, and future asset classes (starting with just prediction markets). Filters should include track-record length, return, worst drawdown, risk, Sharpe ratio, and capacity. Rankings should emphasize risk-adjusted return, consistency, low drawdown, and verified history instead of simply ranking the highest returns.
- Strategy Pages: Each strategy page should work like a simple investment diligence page. Show verified returns, benchmark comparisons, drawdowns, monthly performance, risk metrics, capacity, creator information, and how the record is verified. Losses and worst periods should be shown alongside gains.
- Tailing: Investors should be able to choose a connected account, set an allocation, set a maximum drawdown limit, review the performance fee and high-water mark, and confirm that Tails cannot withdraw their funds.
- Portfolio: Show portfolio value, P&L, returns, strategy allocations, current and historical drawdown, fees, activity, and portfolio-level risk metrics.
- Creators and Social: Creator profiles should show verified strategies, track record, followers, posts, and capital tailing each strategy. Creator onboarding should emphasize that Tails verifies outcomes without requiring creators to reveal their strategy or code. In the social feed, verified performance context should follow creators and appear alongside their posts.
- Trust and Research: Include dedicated Verification, Transparency, and Research sections. Verification explains how records are calculated. Transparency publishes platform-wide performance, losses, and failed strategies. Research teaches users how to evaluate track records and risk.
- Visual Direction: I want the site to feel clean and trustworthy. I like Ridge Wallet (clean, minimal) and Kickstarter's consumer/creator toggle. I do not want it to be gambly"- looking. We should use other similar platforms for reference: freeport markets, robinhood, polymarket. Website should feel like a verified financial marketplace and social network built around proof, transparency, and investor control. Avoid neon colors, casino-style imagery, stock photography, fabricated social proof, and try to avoid overly signal-group language.
- Core UI Rule: Verification comes first. Whenever Tails shows an attractive return figure, it should also show the risk and drawdown required to achieve it.
