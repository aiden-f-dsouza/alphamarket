Three steps. Nothing to explain.

01

Connect a read-only key

From your venue account. We check its scope on the spot: a key that can trade is refused. It is encrypted at rest and erased when you disconnect.

02

We compute and publish the record

Fills, settlements and fees flow into an append-only ledger, reconciled against your balance every night. After 90 days of incubation the page goes live on the marketplace.

03

Investors tail you. You get paid on their profit.

Set your capacity. Investors mirror from their own accounts. You earn a performance fee on the gains above each investor's high-water mark.

Fees, in one line You earn 20% of an investor's profit above their high-water mark. Koine keeps a quarter of that.

No listing fee, no subscription. An investor who is under water pays nothing, so neither of us is paid until they recover. Tailing and fees launch after the verification layer; connecting a key today starts your verified window.
