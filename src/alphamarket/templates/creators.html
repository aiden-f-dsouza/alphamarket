Three steps. Nothing to explain.

01

Connect read-only

An API key, an OAuth approval, a report token or a public wallet address, depending on the venue. We prove it cannot trade on the spot: one that can is refused. Credentials are encrypted at rest and erased when you disconnect.

02

We compute and publish the record

Fills, settlements and fees flow into an append-only ledger, reconciled against your balance every night. After 90 days of incubation the page goes live on the marketplace.

03

Followers paper-tail you. You earn a flat royalty.

Followers copy your new trades with paper money at real venue prices, so they see what following you is worth before they risk anything. Your royalty is flat: the same whether they are up or down.

Pricing, in one line Followers pay a flat subscription. Creators earn a flat royalty. Nobody is paid a share of returns.

No listing fee and no performance fee. Pay never depends on how a strategy performs, so nothing rewards swinging for the fences. Billing launches after the verification layer; connecting today starts your verified window.

Already have a record elsewhere? We can only verify what the venue can show us. Your verified window starts the day you connect. Older activity is imported where the venue allows and is labelled by how far back the data reaches.
