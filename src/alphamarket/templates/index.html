How it works

Proof first. Then, and only then, capital.

Every strategy on Koine earns its place with a record we computed ourselves. Returns are never shown without the drawdown it took to get them.

Read the methodology
01

A creator connects a read-only key

Koine checks the key's scope before storing it. A key that can place orders is refused. Fills and settlements stream into an append-only ledger.

02

The ledger is reconciled every night

We replay every trade and compare the computed balance against the venue's. If they disagree, the page is quarantined until they match.

03

You tail with limits you set

Pick an allocation and a maximum drawdown. Mirrored trades land in your own account. Breach the limit and tailing stops.
