How it works

Proof first. Then a paper tail. Then your call.

Every strategy on Koine earns its place with a record we computed ourselves. Returns are never shown without the drawdown it took to get them, and a paper tail shows what copying it is worth after spreads and fees.

Read the methodology
01

A creator connects read-only

An API key, an OAuth approval, a report token or a public wallet address, depending on the venue. Koine proves it cannot trade before storing it; one that can is refused. Fills and settlements stream into an append-only ledger.

02

The ledger is reconciled every night

We replay every trade and compare the computed balances against the venue's. If they disagree, the page is quarantined until they match.

03

You paper-tail with limits you set

Pick a paper allocation and a maximum drawdown. Each new trade is copied at the venue's quote when Koine saw it (the creator's own price on prediction markets), after the venue's fees, funding and dividends. Breach the limit and copying stops.
